Local Privacy & Speed

Everything runs on your local machine using PyTorch. Radiographs are never stored, logged, or sent to cloud servers.

Why an ensemble?

Individual architectures possess distinct inductive biases: ResNet50 provides deep residual representations, VGG19 stabilizes spatial textures, and EfficientNet & MobileNet calibrate fine boundaries. Averaging their probabilities prevents individual failure modes.
